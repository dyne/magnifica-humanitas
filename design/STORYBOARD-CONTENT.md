# Real-content storyboard script

This is a development prototype using accepted editorial model text, not the
complete public library. Its two-step trail is a proposed demonstration sequence,
not a published trail or a claim of learner testing. Every substantive statement
below is either drawn from the accepted model or labelled editorial interpretation.

## Entry

H1: Explore the ideas in Magnifica Humanitas.
Instruction: Choose a concept and follow its connections.
Direct links: Human dignity (`dignity`), Human rights (`rights`), Common good
(`common-good`), AI governance (`ai-governance`). The full implementation offers

Map/list are equivalent. No concept is selected initially.

## Concept detail

H1: Human dignity. Source-linked **paraphrase**:

> Intrinsic human worth, in the encyclical, belongs to every person before
> usefulness, achievement, social recognition or moral success.

Evidence: §§50–53, 58, 154. Necessary distinction, **editorial interpretation**:

> Moral conduct, social conditions and experienced worth can change; ontological
> dignity cannot be lost. The source grounds it in divine creation and love.

The qualification is visible in the first reading sequence. Optional theological
foundations are a labelled destination, not a silent premise replacement.
Connections expose the real relationship labels and paragraph evidence.

## Trail step

Question: Why is worth not a reward? Demonstration step 1 of 2: Human dignity.
Show the same full idea and necessary distinction, source links, Next: Human
rights, Skip step, Open full concept, Save place, and Return to trails.
Step 2 uses the accepted rights definition and normative distinction, including
the source's attributed abortion/euthanasia position; no secularization erases it.
The ending says “You explored Human dignity and Human rights” with Save/Atlas
links. It does not certify agreement, completion of reflection or mastery.

Optional reflection (editorial interpretation, §52): “Which meanings of dignity
can change in the source's account?” Feedback: moral conduct, social conditions
and felt worth can change; ontological dignity is the source's unconditional
claim. Reveal explanation/Skip are visible buttons. No scoring or answer storage.

## Source context

H1: Source context · §52. Attribution: Leo XIV, Magnifica Humanitas, 15 May 2026.
Edition `mh-en-owner-2026`, PDF page 12. Show the dignity distinction before
this exact accepted quotation:

> These aspects of dignity can be enhanced or diminished.

Label **exact source quotation**, §52, offsets 457–512, original PDF page 12.
The contextual explanation clarifies that “these aspects” refers to moral,
social and existential dignity; the source distinguishes unconditional
ontological dignity. Notes 60 and 61 have named links. Optional foundations,
method/exclusions and the full official original have distinct labels.
Return to Human dignity restores its invoking source link and reading position.
This view does not render all raw source text or splice a redacted quotation.

## Return

H1: Your saved places. Demo state: Human dignity saved at the idea block on this
device; Resume and Remove are explicit. A removal offers Undo. The empty state
has “No saved places yet”, Atlas and Read links. Local-only notice accompanies
the state. The prototype represents one known place; production persistence,
migration and failure handling remain the route/state contract's later gates.

## Complete text path

Entry HTML concept link → concept article → named relationship → endpoint
article → paragraph context → return link → invoking source link. The SVG is
never required for this path. Trail step → full concept and return remain one
history step; reflection never gates Next. Saved → Resume → stable reading block.
Global Read continuously reaches the adapted argument independently of these
paths; it remains in the final production navigation even if the prototype
shows only this neighborhood.
