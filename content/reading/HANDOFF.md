# L4 content handoff to L5

L4 supplies the complete authored English content, not a claim that every new
surface is already rendered. Root review governs acceptance. Source and
editorial artifacts remain frozen; stable IDs are retained. No generated Org
coordination or browser artifact is a Git deliverable.

   `readingEstimate`, finite steps, conclusion, `relatedTrailIds` and

   Its format is `think-then-reveal`, not a required submitted answer. Explicitly

   assign agreement/mastery scores or make answers unlock content.

   Source context and optional foundations exclude performed devotional forms,

## Estimates and performance

`estimateTrailReading` in `scripts/reading-content.mjs` counts the full concept
and relationship prose displayed at each step, transitions, introduction,
qualification, ending and optional reflection. Source and foundation detours
and navigation are excluded. At an assumed 200 whitespace-separated words per
minute, estimates are rounded up. Current totals range from 2,004 to 2,659
words and 11 to 14 estimated minutes. These are unmeasured estimates, not
learning promises. Recompute if L5 changes display scope.

The current project-subpath build has **170.52 kB gzip initial JS**, exceeding
the proposed 150 KiB budget, plus an 89.63 kB gzip deferred context chunk. The
initial import of the expanded JSON includes content not needed on the atlas.
L5 should separate or defer continuous/context/other inactive-route data, keep
raw paragraphs outside the app and measure root/subpath production builds
again. Preserve content depth while changing delivery; do not shorten the
source argument to reach the budget. This is an explicit outstanding obligation,
not a claim that the L4 performance target passed.
