# L4.2 authored trail comparison

Writer evidence for root review. All six trails were composed from the research
questions and the frozen, source-compared L4.1 library. Introductions,
transitions, qualifications, endings, prompts and feedback carry their own
paragraph references and are labelled editorial interpretation. Four scenarios
are explicitly fictional editorial scenarios, not reports of events. No
quotation, probability, measured outcome or external factual update was added.

Reflection feedback explains a distinction rather than evaluates a reader:

- Dignity retrieval: changing conduct, treatment or felt worth does not reduce
  intrinsic worth. Unemployment is not inferior humanity; disagreement with
  the religious grounding remains possible.
- Appeal transfer: individual recourse is a real improvement but does not by
  itself give affected people influence over initial objectives and standards.
- Work transfer: emergency income support meets material need but may not
  restore relationships and contribution. No claim that all tasks should be
  preserved, or that one transition response works everywhere.
- Truth retrieval: verification and the conditions of selection, education and
  institutional transparency are complementary. Theology is not fact-checking.
- Repair transfer: acknowledgment may accept responsibility, while voice,
  continuing consequences, material reparation and prevention remain tasks.
  Historical qualification does not cancel institutional complicity.
- Control transfer: an operator's presence proves neither informed control nor
  adequate time and civilian safeguards. Supporting values work does not lift
  the source's prohibition on delegation of lethal decisions.

The fictional employers, institution and procurement committee are unnamed.
Each scenario links to the passages whose distinction it illustrates and
avoids invented evidence, counts and forecasts. No response is stored in the
content model; no score, mastery claim, locked content or required reflection
is permitted by the validator. Current step views remain ordinary links with
skip and a finite ending; L5 implements the optional reflection reveal and
related choices.

The openly assumed rate is 200 whitespace-separated words per minute, rounded
up. Counts include all substantive prose in each full concept step and its
visible relationship explanations, plus introduction, qualification,
transitions, ending and optional reflection. Source detours, navigation and
optional foundation pages are excluded. The estimates are not measured reader
behavior, timers or a claim that a person understands the argument in that
period. Reading and thought may take longer, and stopping at any step remains
legitimate. L5 should recompute if its display scope changes.

Focused content checks protect identities, citations, source distinctions,
fictional labels, no scoring/unlocking and estimate sensitivity to actual prose.
Those checks are not an automated semantic proof or a reader study. Root's
review remains the acceptance gate for the uncommitted L4 diff.
