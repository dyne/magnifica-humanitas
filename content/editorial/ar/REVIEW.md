# Arabic edition review

## L11.1 — Terminology and editorial alignment

Reviewed on 15 October 2026 against all **245 complete paragraphs and 224 notes**
of the frozen official Arabic HTML edition, `content/source/ar/corpus.json`.
The official HTML is the quotation authority. Its linked PDF is comparison
material: Arabic PDF layout extraction rearranges script, separates diacritics
and introduces 13,720 directional controls. No quotation here is a model
translation or a repair of that extraction.

`alignment-review.json` records **245 individual coverage instructions, 27 concept
terms, nine typed relation labels, 87 complete optional-premise contexts, 121
qualification instructions, 18 exact devotional exclusions, 83 protected contiguous
argument spans and 224 intentional note mappings**. There are 42 contextual
paragraph dispositions and no whole-paragraph devotional omission. Every span
has local UTF-16 coordinates, an exclusive end, exact text, body hash and span
hash. All coordinates were derived from actual Arabic corpus strings; foreign
coordinates were not transferred. Complete source paragraphs bound every exclusion
and faith-premise review. Protected spans are separately contiguous and never
intersect exclusions; they are not concatenated into an apparent quotation.

The English coverage instructions are inherited editorial policy, compared
individually with the Arabic edition. Local corrections are marked explicitly.
The accepted prior schema supplies stable identities and workflow conventions,
not Arabic textual authority. English internal instructions are not public Arabic
prose. These source-comparison records prepare L11.2; they do not publish or enable
Arabic, establish native-speaker proofreading, or prove that later adaptations
preserve meaning.

### Terms and local differences

- §52 calls the variable experienced dimension **كرامة الحياة** and the
  inalienable intrinsic dimension **الكرامة الوجوديّة**. Keep these separate from
  **الكرامة الأخلاقيّة** and **الكرامة الاجتماعيّة**. Sin, failure, humiliation
  and exclusion do not diminish intrinsic worth. Divine willing, creation and
  love remain attributed grounds, accessible as optional foundations.
- Subsidiarity is **اللامركزيّة والتّكامل في اتّخاذ القرار** (§68), a descriptive
  expression rather than a short imported technical name. §69 explicitly rejects
  State indifference; support, coordination, safeguards and prior participation
  remain necessary. §13 describes cooperative efforts without explicitly using
  this technical expression. §31 reverses the order of its two descriptive parts.
- **الغاية الشّاملة للخيرات** includes material, immaterial and cultural goods;
  recognized private property remains subordinate (§§65–67). **المساءلة** (§105)
  includes identification, justification, monitoring, challenge and remedy;
  §86 uses the vocalized spelling **المُساءلة** in ecclesial self-examination.
- §99’s effective computational capabilities coexist with its categorical
  experience/conscience/understanding denials. Those remain the author’s claims;
  §§97–98 expressly limit the treatment and warn that it dates quickly.
- §§108 and 111 locally name communities of believers and affected Christian
  communities. Do not manufacture a universally worded source quotation from
  a different edition. Public practical implications still need their full
  context and the source’s attributed premises.
- §116 uses **ما بعد الإنسانيّة** for technical enhancement and **ما يتجاوز
  الإنسانيّة** for the more radical hybridization account. Follow the actual
  local descriptions, preserving diversity, radical-only qualifications and
  speculative status; do not silently swap labels to fit an external taxonomy.
- §118 says **يجب علينا أن نسعى إلى إزالة الألم**: an obligation to strive to
  remove suffering. Mere permission to alleviate pain would weaken it. Preserve
  this together with intrinsic finitude, the faith-grounded account of contingency
  and §§120/126’s argument. No refusal of treatment follows. Grace (§§127–128)
  remains a gift, not technically achievable enhancement.
- §133’s quoted self-creation/egoistic-closure diagnosis does not literally name
  original sin. Its truth, conscience and inviolable sacred dignity claims still
  need attribution; importing a foreign clause would invent Arabic source text.
- §169’s three terms are **العمل والخدمات والرّاحة**: work, services and rest.
  Do not substitute leisure for services. §143 expressly retains parents’ primary,
  inalienable educational choice, including moral, cultural and religious views.
- §176 explicitly admits delayed condemnation, ecclesial slave ownership,
  regulatory legitimation, nineteenth-century formal absolute universal
  condemnation and eighteen centuries, then requests pardon in the Church’s
  name. Caution against anachronism does not diminish these admissions. Note 175
  separately preserves the 1866 qualification.
- §198 positively values embedding values and prudent judgment in programming,
  moral ecology, conscience and suitable model boundaries. There is no local
  “as far as possible” clause. Preserve this beside categorical non-delegation
  of lethal or irreversible decisions and §§199–200’s effective safeguards.
- §215’s question **لماذا تريد أنت أن تتعارض مع العدل؟** is coherent; Russian’s
  grammatical defect does not belong here. Local Psalm **85، 11** and Augustine’s
  n191 citation on Psalm 84,12 are distinct from note n084, linked only to §64.
- §237’s quoted label is **المركزيّة البشريّة**, without a “situated” adjective;
  the following relational creaturehood definition must survive. §241 names
  **الإصغاء والشّجاعة** (listening and courage), not another edition’s obedience.
- Frozen anomalies remain untouched: isolated **مليار** at the end of §51,
  **عنما** opening §128, and §159’s affirmative **إنّها تُبَيِّن الأنشطة**.
  Disclose tensions rather than fabricate a repaired exact quotation. §66’s
  empty duplicate `_ftnref71` remains source metadata and creates no visible
  note reference or n071 backlink.

The nine relationship labels are editorial verbs, with independently selected
Arabic source anchors. They are not represented as the author’s own taxonomy.
All 27 concept labels have a local exact anchor and an explicit distinction or
review instruction; descriptive editorial labels are identified in their records.

## L11.2 — Arabic reading, concepts and trails

### Independent Source explanations

`Source.vue` renders the built context explanation independently. Every one of

The source-only regression suite checks **25 sensitive explanations** in the

meaning witnesses. These checks guard particular losses; they are not a universal
semantic validator. All 245 explanations and 60 pairs are separately hash-bound
