## Terminology and relation labels

The 27-concept map uses **godność człowieka**, **prawa człowieka**, **dobro
wspólne**, **powszechne przeznaczenie dóbr**, **pomocniczość**, **solidarność**,
**sprawiedliwość społeczna** and **integralny rozwój człowieka**. Use **AI**, as
the edition does. **Troska i bliskość** covers embodied relationships and presence,
beyond medical treatment. **Ciało i skończoność**, **wolność i sprawczość** and
**niewidzialna praca i wyzysk** are explanatory navigation labels, with separate
source anchors rather than invented official quotations.

**Rozliczalność** is anchored to §86's *rozliczalności*. §105 retains the English
word *accountability* and explains who must **zdać sprawę**, justify decisions,
submit them to scrutiny and challenge, and repair harm. Preserve that fuller
meaning rather than replacing it with generic responsibility. §107 calls
alignment **dostosowanie**; technical adjustment does not decide whose values
govern a system. §237's **antropocentryzm umiejscowiony** places people within
relations with living beings and creation.

All nine relation types have Polish editorial verbs and exact local evidence.
They describe normative grounding, requirement, conditional support,
qualification, application, tension, a test and two directed foundation
relations. These verbs are interpretations, not quotations or empirical causal
claims. Stable endpoints and optional-foundations placement remain required.

## Exclusions, notes and edition differences

The **18 exclusions** bind exact local forms to their complete paragraph
contexts and preservation instructions. They remove performed invocation,
blessing, liturgical responses, ceremonial address, canticle recitation and
Marian entrustment. The small coordinated clause **modlitwy i** in §241 alone
uses reviewed `source-location` matching; it cannot be treated as a global ban
on ordinary bibliographic references to prayer. Other complete forms retain
global matching. Never concatenate surviving clauses into an apparent quotation.

Nehemiasz's reported prayer (§§8, 241), Frankl's **modlitwa Pańska** and **Szema
Jisrael** (§121), honorifics (§125), Benedict's historical combination of prayer
and work (§148), invocations of ethics and institutional pardon (§176) are not
performed devotions. Bibliographic titles naming prayers or blessings remain
exact source records, not devotional UI instructions.

All **224 notes** have local body hashes, visible markers, backlinks, exact
official link metadata and reviewed same-number correspondence. **35** leading
*Tamże / Por. tamże* references identify the preceding work; **23** leading
*Tenże / Por. Tenże* references identify the preceding author, with a different
work often named. These are distinguished, not interpreted through an English
*ibid.* regex. n163 refers to the closing Benedict XVI citation in n162, rather
than its opening Franciszek citation. n217 points internally to §§11–14 and
does not inherit n216 as a preceding-work reference.

Notes **n132, n174, n175 and n182** contain substantive arguments. They preserve
heart, poetry and personal history; four named papal bulls and political/economic
pressure; incomplete condemnation in the 1866 Holy Office instruction; and strict
defense conditions with the preventive-war warning. n143 remains an Arendt
bibliography with both original and Polish editions, not the German expanded
argument. n028 and n068 explicitly give differing paragraph numbering within
the cited Polish *Pacem in terris*. Do not confuse that numbering with this
document's stable IDs. n213 retains its local December 2005 date and AAS 98
(2006) citation rather than another edition's anomaly.

The **41 edition-difference records** also preserve §61's `[wł. plus]`, §130's
city/state gloss, §164's frozen typo **inkulzywność**, §169's **usługi**, §234's
**1 Kor 12, 27** and local terminology. Editorial prose may use correct spelling
without inventing a repaired source quotation. Polish n033 has official name
`_ft33`; n219 visibly labels `[219]` and belongs to §237 but repeats name
`_ftn210` and backlink `#_ftnref210`. Their metadata remains exact; local IDs
stay `n033` and `n219`. Never guess repaired Vatican fragment destinations.
