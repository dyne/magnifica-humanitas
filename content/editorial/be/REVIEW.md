### §176 — a substantive edition tension

The frozen sentence says:

> Аднак мы не можам адмаўляць ці змяншаць той факт, што і грамадства, і Касцёл асудзілі зло рабства.

Unlike the corresponding English statement, this sentence does not specify
**delay** in condemnation. Later in the same paragraph the Belarusian explicitly
says **Касцёл доўга цярпеў рабства**, identifies eighteen centuries before the
formal declaration of incompatibility, admits slave-owning Church institutions
and legitimation of subjugation, and ends with an institutional request for
pardon. Note 175 explicitly records incomplete condemnation even in 1866.
The complete paragraph is protected. Do not silently insert a missing qualifier
into a quotation, remove the tension from the source view or use that sentence
to cancel the later admissions. A faithful labelled paraphrase can explain delay
from the paragraph’s explicit later wording and note 175. Pardon addressed to
those harmed is institutional accountability, not a prayer to remove.
