# German source alignment and authoring handoff

`alignment-review.json` is internal L4.1 editorial evidence, bound to the frozen
German edition and its HTML SHA-256. It is not a public reading bundle. All 245
complete German paragraphs were read. Relevant notes were compared in full for
terminology, qualifications, institutional criticism and devotional exclusions.
The 224-note inventory verifies local numbering, markers, hashes, paragraph
backlinks and preceding-citation dependencies; it does not claim completed public
commentary on every note. L4.2 must review all public prose and screen every note.
No native-speaker proofreading, reader study or independent scientific, clinical,
historical or legal verification is claimed.

The 27 concept identities and all nine directed relation types retain the
accepted model. German labels are editorial display wording; exact source
anchors are separate, contiguous spans with local UTF-16 coordinates, paragraph
hashes and span hashes. Relation verbs interpret reasoning rather than quote it
or claim empirical causation. Public prose should use **KI**, **Gemeinwohl**,
**allgemeine Bestimmung der Güter**, **Subsidiarität**, **Solidarität**,
**ganzheitliche menschliche Entwicklung** and **Fürsorge** consistently.
**Accountability** is the source's loanword in §105, explained there as
**Rechenschaft ablegen**: identifiable responsibility, reasons, control,
contestability and remedy. **Handlungsfähigkeit** is an editorial explanation of
agency; it does not add a new source doctrine. **Leiblichkeit** keeps the embodied
person distinct from a body treated as optimizable material.

The coverage inventory preserves 137 retain, 66 adapt and 42 optional-context
decisions. No paragraph is wholly omitted. Internal English authoring instructions
reuse the accepted editorial policy, cross-checked against the complete German
paragraphs, with explicit changes where their emphasis or details would mislead.
They must never be rendered as German public prose. Each record uses its German
source hash. Optional faith-premise records bind the existing attributed premise
instructions to complete German paragraph review contexts. These are conservative
review boundaries, not exact extracts approved for public quotation or a method
for automatically slicing theological and secular sentences apart.

Eighteen devotional/ceremonial forms were located individually, each with a full
local paragraph as review context and a preservation instruction. Sixty-four
protected spans keep qualifications and arguments distinct from those exclusions.
They include the rights positions (§55), restricted and rapidly changing KI
account (§§97–99), narrow legitimate defence (§192), and all of §198's affirmative
final sentence on values, prudent judgement, a moral ecosystem, human conscience
and model restrictions. Never concatenate surviving fragments into a quotation.

Only §241's incomplete coordinated fragment **Gebet und** has
`matchScope: source-location`, with an explicit functional review. It is part of
the reader-facing exhortation near the end, not an independently performable
prayer. The reported prayer earlier in that paragraph remains narrative context.
All complete invocations, blessings and responses retain global matching. A
`clause` classification alone does not authorize narrowing the matching scope.
No runtime validator changes are part of this handoff.

The source comparison preserves these argument pairs and boundaries:

- §§1–45: humane versus dehumanizing construction; technology's possibilities and
  uncertain consequences; shared planning and plurality; the Church's public

- §§90–130: useful KI capabilities and the author's categorical claims about

  warn that technical claims date quickly. Justice starts in design, participation

  accepting finitude is not refusing treatment. Grace, Incarnation and Trinity

- §§131–181: factual verification and relational trust both matter; truth is not
  whatever power declares. Journalism exposing Church abuse does not transfer

  development is distinct from finance for itself; justice belongs within

- §§182–228: hybrid conflict and dual-use ambiguity coexist with possible civilian

  judgement in programming, an ethical setting for people to heed conscience and
  restrictions in models. Identifiable responsibility, judgement time, civilian

  agency does not imply equal power or replace institutions. Operational
  humanitarian neutrality (§123) differs from moral indifference (§216). Memory
  serves peace rather than revenge. Healthy realism, negotiations and deep
  multilateral reform are demanding possibilities, not guaranteed successes.
  §228's unarmed, disarming, humble and persevering peace is an ethical ideal to
  preserve in attributed paraphrase even though its performed blessing is excluded.
- §§229–245: sacramental unity and truthful assent retain their theological and

## L4.2 public reading comparison

The complete German authored library is `content/reading/de.json`, revision
`de-2026-10-15-reading-2`. Local `coverage.json`, `concepts.json` and
`relations.json` provide its executable contract. These four artifacts contain
German public prose; the earlier alignment instructions remain internal English.
All source quotations come directly from the unchanged German corpus. No source
passage was translated from English or Italian. The canonical English library
supplies stable identities and citation groupings, not public wording.

All 245 complete German paragraphs and all 224 German notes were read. The
paragraph explanations were authored individually from those source passages.
They supply 203 main sequential entries, 42 optional-context destinations and a
separate source-context explanation for every paragraph. Eighty-one individually
written premise explanations support source contexts and optional foundations.
Contexts add a theological supplement only where the paragraph explanation does
not already give that account, avoiding repeated assembled summaries. Necessary
qualifications remain visible in main prose rather than hidden behind a link.
The source-context review bounds in the ledger remain whole local paragraphs;
they are not slicing instructions or permission to publish excluded forms.

All 27 concepts retain their 86 stable reasoning-block IDs. Reasoning deliberately
reuses the individually authored German paragraph explanations with their actual
references, grouping those arguments under the stable concept blocks. It does not
use a glossary substitution or a repeated generic explanation. Definitions,
necessary distinctions and optional foundations are separate German prose. All
44 directed relations preserve endpoints, types and placement, with individual
German explanations of the connection. Foundation relations remain optional.

Six trails preserve all 32 step identities, transitions, endings, related paths
and voluntary reflections. Four reflections have explicitly fictional scenarios;
none invents real institutions, outcome numbers or reported events. Questions
ask readers to explain distinctions, never to agree with religious or political
positions. Current estimates count the rendered authored prose at an assumed
200 words/minute: 17, 21, 16, 17, 17 and 24 minutes in canonical trail order.
These are estimates, not measured reading times or evidence of learning.

The comparison checked agency, causation, polarity, certainty, conditions,
scope and attribution. Particular visible safeguards include:

- Four dignity meanings and the difference between changes in conduct, treatment
  or self-perception and inalienable personal worth. Work expresses this worth
  without conferring it; emergency support and participation without work remain.
- The Church's life-from-conception-to-natural-end, abortion/euthanasia and
  man–woman family positions remain attributed, as does parents' primary and
  inalienable educational choice. The source does not require reader assent.
- Property is recognized and socially limited. Subsidiarity demands public
  support, corporate checks and participation before standards are fixed. The
  source's call for common or collective data management remains explicit.
- Beneficial KI capabilities coexist with the author's categorical denials of
  experience and conscience. These are attributed claims, not scientific consensus;
  §§97–98 limit scope and warn that technical descriptions date quickly.
- Different enhancement currents, radical variants and speculative claims are
  distinguished. The duty to relieve suffering remains explicit, and acceptance
  of finitude does not become refusal of treatment. Philosophical reflection
  on pain is not clinical guidance; grace is not technical self-improvement.
- Truth requires verification and relationships; power cannot make a claim true.
  The Church's own transparency duties survive journalists' contribution to
  exposing abuse. Schools' access, pedagogical and intellectual challenges remain
  different, and responsibility for child protection is not placed only on parents.
- Desired relief from dangerous work, unequal transitions, civic participation,
  justice within production and the useful social function of credit remain
  distinct from employment protection, redistribution and speculative finance.
- Church slaveholding, legitimation, delayed condemnation and pardon remain
  explicit alongside historical context. Notes 174–175 retain the named bulls,
  political/economic pressures and the 1866 qualification. This is institutional
  self-criticism, not a prayer to remove.
- Narrow last-resort defence, non-delegation of lethal/irreversible decisions,
  values and prudent judgement in programming, a moral ecosystem, human conscience,
  model restrictions, time for judgement, civilians and effective conscious
  human control remain together. A nominal confirmation button is insufficient.
- Hope does not guarantee progress or erase unequal power. Humanitarian operational
  neutrality differs from indifference to attacks on civilians. Memory supports
  peace rather than revenge; healthy realism and negotiations preserve uncertainty.
- The conclusion retains physical care, hidden labour, action rather than spiritual
  retreat and everyday sharing. The political conditions in §243 remain oppressive
  despite Mary's changed perspective. Widows, orphans, strangers, wounded children,
  exiles and refugees in §244 remain visible after devotional forms are excluded.
