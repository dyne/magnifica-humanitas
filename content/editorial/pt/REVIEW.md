# Portuguese terminology and source-alignment handoff

L7.1 compares the complete 245 paragraphs and 224 notes of the frozen official
Portuguese edition, `mh-pt-official-html-2026-10-14`. `alignment-review.json` binds
that comparison to the local HTML hash, paragraph hashes and exact UTF-16 spans.
This is internal authoring evidence for L7.2, not published reading content,
native-speaker proofreading, an external factual update or an efficacy study.

The 27-term map uses **dignidade humana**, **bem comum**, **destinação universal
dos bens**, **subsidiariedade**, **solidariedade**, **justiça social** and
**desenvolvimento humano integral**. Use **IA**. Care is **cuidado e proximidade
humana**, broader than clinical treatment. **Corpo e finitude** and **liberdade
e capacidade de agir** are explanatory editorial labels. In §105 the edition
explicitly names **responsabilização (accountability)**, then explains identifying
who must **prestar contas**, justify decisions, submit to control and contestation,
and repair damage. This differs from French §105's generic responsabilité.
The term map and nine relation verbs are editorial navigation wording; their
separate exact source anchors do not turn editorial labels into quotations.
