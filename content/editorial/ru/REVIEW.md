and love premise remains attributed. **Подотчетность** (§105) is more precise

permit checking and challenge, and repair harm. It applies to the Church itself
(§86), as well as technological actors. **Выравнивание** (§107) names AI
alignment locally; it cannot replace public deliberation over the ethical code.

proof. Relation type, direction and endpoints remain stable for L10.2 authoring.

### Local corrections and frozen differences

The local comparison corrects inherited policy where its detail does not match:

- §47 addresses lay faithful, all people of good will, academics and universities,
  with research supporting the pastoral/teaching task. It is not merely a division
  between lay people and pastoral leaders.
- §63 warns against short-term calculation and sterile polarization. It does not
  make the inherited paragraph-specific corruption claim.
- §82 has local n110. The English PDF/web extra-marker discrepancy is not a Russian
  discrepancy and must not be imported.
- §118 says **мы обязаны стремиться устранить страдание**. Preserve the duty to
  strive to eliminate suffering beside wise acknowledgment of finitude. Do not
  substitute the weaker formulation of another edition, imply inevitable success,
  or turn acceptance of limits into refusal of treatment. §120’s philosophical
  argument about pain, love and desire must remain connected to this care duty.
- §133 explicitly calls the original-sin explanation language of faith. It adds
  an independently reviewed optional premise.
  The practical account of power, conscience, universal dignity and law must
  remain intelligible independently. §§13, 74 and 90 also have locally explicit
  Pauline, faith or biblical/gift premises beyond the base English inventory.
- §112 contrasts an optimization project with a creature called to communion;
  §147 explicitly includes the sense of the transcendent in educational aims.
  Their religious premises remain optional and attributed beside the independently
  explained social argument. These bring the local total to 87. Ordinary ecology
  uses of creation, institutional identity and religious educational choice are
  not automatically treated as additional theological proofs.
- §143 explicitly retains parents’ primary and inalienable educational choice in
  accordance with moral, cultural and religious convictions.
- §169 uses **трудом, услугами и отдыхом**. Preserve services, together with work
  stability, retraining and supportive networks; do not import another edition’s
  leisure wording.
- §176 explicitly names **насколько запоздало** condemnation came. Preserve that
  delay, slave ownership, regulatory legitimation, nineteenth-century condemnation,
  eighteen centuries and institutional pardon. The Belarusian missing-delay
  tension is absent here. n175 supplies the local 1866 qualification.
- §198 affirmatively requires values and prudent judgment in programming. There
  is no local “as far as possible” clause. This does not create machine conscience
  or permission to delegate lethal/irreversible decisions. The local affirmative
  work and categorical prohibition must remain together.
- §207 concerns moral limits, government advantage, economic calculation, media
  illusions and principles hollowed out by interests. Do not add a separate
  paragraph-specific rights claim from the inherited instruction.
- §215’s **Почему же ты приемлешь справедливости?** is grammatically defective.
  Keep it exact in quotation and disclose the tension; do not insert a guessed
  negation. The surrounding argument clearly joins justice and peace through
  prohibitions on theft/adultery and reciprocal treatment. Psalm **84(85)** is
  scriptural numbering, not n084; Augustine’s source is n191. n084 links only §64.

Other frozen anomalies include §4’s **в ещах**, §78’s **культуру [106]отбросов**,
§198’s **руководствоваться совесть**, §201’s **единство и миро**, §213’s
**я предлагаю способов**, and missing spaces in several bibliographic titles
and n175. Exact source witnesses retain all of them. Authored explanations may
use grammatical Russian but cannot present repairs as official quotations.
The Russian structural alignment has no recorded official-link anomalies;
Polish duplicate-link and Belarusian Psalm-note defects must not be imported.

## Exclusions and optional foundations

All 245 paragraphs retain a disposition; none is wholly omitted. The 18 local
exclusions are invocations, performed prayer, renewed blessing, ceremonial
address, liturgical responses, praise recital and final Marian entrustment.
Each has a complete local review context and a specific preservation instruction.
Protected spans are contiguous, never assembled by joining surviving fragments.
An excluded form may carry thought that must survive in labelled paraphrase.

In particular, §228 retains the **безоружный**, **обезоруживающий**,
**смиренный** and **терпеливый** peace ideal, plus work in relationships and
society. §§231–235 retain vulnerable people, Incarnation, sacramental membership,
solidarity and social obligations. §241 excludes only the final coordinated
**молитву и** component: adjoining responsibility survives. This incomplete
fragment has reviewed `source-location` matching; other complete devotional
forms remain global. Nehemiah’s earlier reported prayer, Frankl’s historical
victims, Benedict’s prayer/work relationship, historical titles and the Church’s
request for pardon are not reader-performed prayers to delete.

§243 preserves unchanged Roman domination and a changed interpretive viewpoint.
§244 keeps widow, orphan, stranger, wounded child, exile and refugee in the main
argument. §245 keeps sharing oneself and one’s goods and daily fidelity around
the excluded final entrustment. Optional foundations identify faith premises;
they do not authorize deleting their surrounding ethical or institutional work.

For L10.2, every source context must independently explain its paragraph’s core
argument, qualifications and attributed optional premise. `Source.vue` renders
that explanation without combining it with the main sequence. Neither a main
entry, placement rationale, concept quotation nor repeated premise appendix can
repair an incomplete standalone explanation. Audit all main/foundation pairs
and test the built context explanations separately.

### Substantive decisions

- All four dignity meanings remain separate. Work expresses dignity without
  conferring intrinsic worth; accountability for conduct does not erase it.
- Universal rights retain practical guarantees and the attributed Church
  life-from-conception, abortion/euthanasia and family positions. Parents’ primary
  educational choice, women’s equal treatment and migrants’ agency remain explicit.
- Recognized property is subordinated to universal goods; subsidiarity includes
  support and public safeguards and expressly rejects State disengagement.
- AI’s effective capabilities remain beside the author’s categorical claims about
  experience and conscience. The limited, rapidly dating account is not presented
  as scientific consensus. Participation, recourse and justice begin in design.
- §118, concepts, the care relation and the dignity trail retain the Russian
  **obligation to strive to eliminate suffering**, together with finite human
  life, no guarantee of eliminating all pain and no justification for refusing
  treatment. Diverse enhancement currents and radical/speculative qualifications
  remain visible; grace is not technical self-upgrading.
- Church self-scrutiny includes spiritual, economic, institutional and sexual
  abuse, power and conscience, attention to victims, reparation, prevention,
  common resources and corrective evaluation. Journalists’ contribution does not
  transfer the Church’s duty of openness to outsiders.
- §176 retains explicit delay, slave ownership, regulatory legitimation, formal
  complete universal condemnation in the nineteenth century, eighteen centuries,
  n175’s 1866 qualification and institutional pardon. The caution against
  anachronism does not minimize these admissions. No Belarusian wording tension

- Work retains necessary emergency aid, desirable relief from arduous/repetitive/

- Strict legitimate defense and last resort coexist with non-delegation of lethal
