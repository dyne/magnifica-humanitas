# Spanish source alignment and authoring handoff

`alignment-review.json` is internal L5.1 evidence for the frozen official Spanish
edition, bound to its HTML hash. All 245 complete paragraphs and all 224 notes
were read in Spanish. The public reading and interface remain L5.2/L5.3 work.
No native-speaker proofreading, external factual update, reader study or
learning-efficacy claim is made.

The term map covers all 27 stable concepts and all nine directed relation types.
Display labels are editorial wording, with separate exact Spanish source anchors.
Use **IA**, **dignidad humana**, **bien común**, **destino universal de los bienes**,
**subsidiariedad**, **solidaridad**, **justicia social**, **desarrollo humano integral**
and **cuidado** consistently. §105 names *responsabilidad (accountability)* and
explains *rendir cuentas*: the editorial label **Rendición de cuentas** identifies
answerability, reasons, control, challenge and remedy, not responsibility in the
abstract. **Cuerpo y finitud** and **capacidad de decidir** explain embodied life
and agency without creating additional source doctrines. Relation labels express
editorial reasoning, not empirical causation or source quotation.

The coverage review retains 137 retain, 66 adapt and 42 optional-context decisions,
with no whole-paragraph omission. Each decision binds its local paragraph hash.
Internal English policy instructions reuse the accepted model after comparison
with the Spanish source; they must never appear as public Spanish explanations.
Eighty-one attributed faith-premise records use whole Spanish paragraphs as
review boundaries. Those boundaries do not authorize automatic slicing or public
quotation of whole mixed passages. Public prose must explain the source directly,

Eighteen devotional/ceremonial forms have exact local UTF-16 offsets, complete

The reported prayer earlier in that paragraph remains narrative. Complete

prayers, blessings, liturgy and devotions in citation titles are not performed
